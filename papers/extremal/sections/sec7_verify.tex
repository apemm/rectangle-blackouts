\section{Computer checks}\label{sec:verify}

The computer-assisted entries of Table~\ref{tab:values} need 2 kinds of finite checks. The
repository~\cite{repo} contains the code and the evidence files. Its supplement
\nolinkurl{papers/extremal/supplement.pdf} describes the SAT encodings, proves that they
are faithful, and lists the instances and the certificates.

\paragraph{Lower bounds.} Every blackout and test board of Section~\ref{sec:small}
was checked from Definition~\ref{def:valid} by at least 2 separately written
programs, which list all rectangles and compare their presentations. One of them is the
script \nolinkurl{papers/extremal/scripts/check_constructions.py}. It also checks the
blackouts of Proposition~\ref{prop:seas} for $3\le h\le8$ and the first-fit blackouts of
Remark~\ref{rem:constants} for $4\le h\le14$ and $h=16$, and it computes the first-fit
widths for $h=20$ and $h=24$. For $h=5$ and $h=7$ the script uses 2 blocks with the sea rows $\{1,h-1\}$ and $\{2,h\}$.
No proof in this paper covers this variant, since Proposition~\ref{prop:partial}(c) needs
$h\ge9$. These 2 heights are extra tests. At $h=16$ all 3 blackouts built with (B1) to (B3) are valid, and all 5 blackouts
built with (B1) alone are not valid.

\paragraph{Upper bounds.} Each upper bound below the row bound is the unsatisfiability of
SAT instances. CaDiCaL~\cite{biere2024} produced an LRAT proof for each of the $739$
instances, and each proof was accepted by 2 checkers, the program \texttt{lrat-check} of
drat-trim~\cite{cruzfilipe2017,wetzler2014} and the LRAT checker of the Lean core library.

\paragraph{Formal proofs.} A Lean development in the repository proves the row bound, the
finite collar theorem, Proposition~\ref{prop:seas} under the stronger assumption
$p_{j+1}-p_j\ge2W_h+2$, and the values $f(5,m)=m+6$ for $m\ge8$ and $f(6,m)=m+9$ for
$m\ge15$. The spacing $W_h+1$ and Section~\ref{sec:threshold} are not formalized.
