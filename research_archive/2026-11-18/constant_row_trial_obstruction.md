# A long mixed collision survives every fixed menu of constant sea rows

The [short-window construction](random_constant_row_windows.md) assigns each diagonal and index type a bounded number of candidate constant odd sea rows. Even if its true-to-true vertical rivals are fully solved, a fixed menu of those constant rows cannot give global validity for nearly all labels in an affine band with a long constant-row exterior. There is a four-black-cell obstruction of span at most \(5h/3\).

Fix an odd true row residue \(a_0\bmod28\) in a band. Every true column is divisible by seven and by two in the band's local coordinates. Consider any \(K\) possible bands for a label, with respective sea rows \(s_1,\ldots,s_K\) as in the construction. Thus \(s_j\equiv a_0+2\bmod4\) and \(s_j\not\equiv a_0\bmod7\). Even if the origin of the true columns varies by band, all differences between true columns in each band are divisible by 14. Suppose each candidate position lies at least \(2h\) columns from a change of the candidate sea or a physical boundary, with all nontrue columns in this interval filled in that candidate row. The [multistride layout](multistride_constant_row_windows.md) can give each of its \(O(h)\) bands that much padding at width \(O(h^2)\).

Choose \(K\) even integers \(m_j\ge2\) such that \(m_j\equiv2\bmod7\) and the odd numbers \(A_j=m_j^2-1\) are pairwise coprime. Construct them successively by requiring \(m_j\) to be divisible by every earlier \(A_i\). Each \(A_j\equiv3\bmod7\) and \(\gcd(A_j,28)=1\), so the requirements are compatible. Write \(B_j=2m_j,\ C_j=m_j^2+1\); then \(A_j^2+B_j^2=C_j^2\), \(7\nmid B_j\), and \(C_j/A_j\le5/3\).

On any interval of \(\gg h\) permissible odd rows in the selected residue class, the simultaneous system
\[
 a\equiv a_0\bmod28,\qquad a\equiv s_j\bmod A_j\quad(1\le j\le K)
\]
has \(\gg_K h\) solutions for sufficiently large \(h\), uniformly in the choices of rows \(s_j\). Fix one. In candidate \(j\), suppress the subscript and put \(k=(a-s)/A\). Then \(k\equiv2\bmod4\), \(7\nmid k\), and \(k\ne0\). If the true point at \(x_0\) is black, consider the following additional points of the sea:
\[
 (x_0+Bk,s),\qquad (x_0+(B-C)k/2,s),\qquad (x_0+(B+C)k/2,s).
\]
The first offset is nonzero modulo seven and the last two are odd, so all three columns are singleton sea columns. The true point and the first sea point form a diagonal of length \(C|k|\). The other two sea points form a horizontal black side of that same length. Their horizontal midpoint coordinates agree. The horizontal segment one half of \(a-s\) above this side has the same midpoint as the diagonal and the same length. Its two endpoints have integer coordinates, and its row is strictly between \(a\) and \(s\), so both competing rectangles lie inside the strip. They are distinct, since one contains an oblique diagonal with nonzero vertical component. Their symmetric difference consists of the four displayed black points, including the true point. Extra black cells anywhere preserve this collision. All the relevant columns belong to an interval of span \(C|k|\le5h/3\), and lie within \(2h\) of \(x_0\).

In fact the same obstruction works at span \((1/2+\epsilon)h\) for every fixed \(\epsilon>0\). Take \(0<\tau<1/12\) small enough that \((1+\tau)(1/2+\tau)<1/2+\epsilon\). In choosing the fixed \(m_j\), require them to be so large that \(C_j/A_j\le1+\tau\). Restrict \(a\) to the interval \([(1/2-\tau)h,(1/2+\tau)h]\), which is available for every positive odd \(T\in[h/8,h/4]\). The Chinese remainder theorem still gives \(\gg_{K,\epsilon}h\) labels per diagonal and type; now \(|a-s_j|\le(1/2+\tau)h\) for every trial row, including boundary rows. The bound follows since the common horizontal span equals \(C_j|a-s_j|/A_j\).

This excludes the label in every candidate band. For a bounded budget per central diagonal and type, a positive proportion of labels must be moved elsewhere. The same conclusion holds for a bounded average number of bands per diagonal: among the \(\gg h\) central diagonals with a linear interval of available rows of the selected type, a positive proportion have a bounded individual budget. The uniform Chinese remainder bound applies to each of those diagonals. It follows that the large-window theorem cannot simply be extended to linear span with these padded constant-row trials and only \(o(h^2)\) exceptions. Shorter exteriors, a sea that varies within the band, or a different construction are outside this obstruction.
