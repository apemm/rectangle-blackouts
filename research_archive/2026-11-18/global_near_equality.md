# Near equality on the full strip before the known threshold

Let \(q_h=\lfloor h^2/4\rfloor\). For every fixed \(\varepsilon>0\), uniformly for
\[
 m\ \ge\ h^2(\log h)^{(\log3)/2+\varepsilon},
\]
one has
\[
 f(h,m)=m+(1-o(1))q_h\qquad(h\to\infty).
\]
In particular the number of missing doubles is \(o(h^2)\) at a smaller logarithmic exponent than the one needed for the full equality in the current stride-menu theorem. The conclusion uses globally valid configurations. It does not show that every label can be inserted.

## Typical diagonals

Write \(L=\log h\). Use the stride-four ideal bound \(\kappa_T\le C(1+\log(3h/|T|))g(|T|)\) of the [lemma on distances and ideals](../../papers/extremal/drafts/upper_bound/part7_heavy.tex), with \(g\) as in the [quadratic-width partial bound](global_partial_size_bound.md). Here \(g(n)\) is the product of \(2e+1\) over the split prime powers \(q^e\) that divide \(n\) to the full power. Discard \(|T|<h/L\), involving \(O(h^2/L)\) labels. Among the remaining diagonals all but \(o(h)\) satisfy
\[
 g(|T|)\le L^{(\log3)/2+\delta}
\]
for any fixed \(\delta>0\). To see this, for a uniform \(n\le h\) count its distinct split prime divisors up to \(h^{1/4}\). The mean is \((1/2)\log\log h+O(1)\) by Mertens' theorem in arithmetic progressions, applied to the two split classes \(\pm1\) modulo five. This is an outside input. The variance is \(O(\log\log h)\): for distinct \(p,q\) the joint divisibility probability is \(1/(pq)+O(1/h)\), and the sum of the floor errors is \(O(h^{-1/2})\). At most three prime factors exceed \(h^{1/4}\). The remaining prime-power excess \(\sum_p\sum_{k\ge2}1_{p^k\mid n}\) has bounded mean, by the convergent sum of \(p^{-k}\). The inequality \(2e+1\le3^e\), Chebyshev's inequality for the distinct factors, and Markov's inequality for the excess factors with a threshold \(K=\sqrt{\log\log h}\), give the assertion. Restriction to odd \(T\) and allowing both signs preserves the exceptional-count bound.

Use \(\delta>0\) small enough that \(s=\lceil L^{(\log3)/2+2\delta}\rceil\) satisfies \(s=o(B)\) in the ordinary one-stride chain and \(2\delta<\varepsilon\). On each retained typical diagonal the degree of the forbidden-distance graph is \(2\kappa_T\le s/4\) for all large \(h\), including the factor \(O(\log\log h)\) from the ideal bound. These diagonals are light in the original construction.

## Using the first \(s\) colors

Remove the fixed boundary rows (R-a), (R-b), (R-c) from the selection. In each compatible diagonal-type pair use the small-shape in-class mixed graphs and \(R_{\rm FF}^+\) from the [partial-size proof](global_partial_size_bound.md). Their total number of edges in that pair is \(O(h)\), uniformly even on short diagonals. The sources do not state this count in this form. It follows from the [structure of the in-class families](../../papers/extremal/drafts/upper_bound/part5_classes.tex) and from the count of \(O(X^{2/3})\) shapes of \(R_{\rm FF}\) in the [table](../../papers/extremal/drafts/upper_bound/part6_table.tex). Delete every vertex of degree exceeding \(s/4\) in this union, at a cost \(O(h/s)\) per pair. The remaining graph, including the forbidden distances, has maximum degree at most \(s/2\). Properly color it with colors \(0,\dots,s-1\). Give the other vertices of this pair only colors \(s,\dots,B-1\), imposing the original forbidden-distance requirements. This extension is possible since its forbidden-distance degree is at most \(s/4\) on the whole typical diagonal and \(s=o(B)\). Use any proper coloring on the other light diagonals before deleting their remaining labels.

The selected vertices cannot belong to the bounded-shape part of \(\mathrm{Bad}_{\rm split}\): on their diagonal and type, no unselected vertex has an early color, and the early colors are proper for all these graphs. Across all types and diagonals, the large-shape and cross-class exceptions (R-a) through (R-f) cost \(O(h^{11/6}\log h+h^{9/5+1/100}+h^{3/2})\), by the [assembly estimates](../../papers/extremal/drafts/upper_bound/part8_assembly.tex) with the minimizing primes. Those estimates are stated for the board of Part 8. They apply here since none of the counts depends on the coloring. The [obstruction-table theorem](../../papers/extremal/drafts/upper_bound/part6_table.tex) fixes the colorings and its proof uses no randomness, so it applies to the prescribed colorings. Its conclusion for the chain is stated for the full set of labels. For a subset, repeat its last paragraph with the closure lemma below. Keep only the surviving early-color selected labels, reverting the other label slots to their original filler. This removal uses the [closure lemma](../../papers/extremal/drafts/upper_bound/part2_validity.tex). The obstruction-table theorem shows that every bad set of the full candidate region meets the relocation set. The closure lemma then shows that no subset of the surviving labels creates a violation upon reversion to the original filler. A bad set is defined with the filler in every other label column, so this covers a collision between the filler of a reverted slot and true points. The sea and the exterior are those of the same chain. We have kept \(q_h-o(h^2)\) labels. Losses from the discarded small and atypical diagonals, the \(O(h^2/s)\) high-degree deletions, and the displayed power-saving errors are each \(o(h^2)\).

## Shortening the empty tails of all regions

The chain has 512 regions. Within each one all surviving labels lie in the first \(s\) color periods. Its slot interval begins at \(b_\tau\) and ends at \(b_\tau+(A-1)+16\ell pB\). Every remaining true label lies strictly before \(b_\tau+(A-1)+16\ell p s\), by the [period rule](../../papers/extremal/drafts/upper_bound/part4_regions.tex). The empty tail is filled with the region's original profile, of period four slots or 16 columns. Keep more than \(2W_h+1\) original columns of this profile on both sides of the deletion. Delete a multiple of 16 columns from the interior of this tail, leaving at most \(4W_h+O(1)\) tail columns in total (or keep the entire tail if shorter). Each new seam and a \(2W_h\) neighborhood on both sides agree with a pure periodic word. That word is a translate of the label-free region, which is clean on the whole strip by the [proposition on the label-free region](../../papers/extremal/drafts/upper_bound/part6_table.tex). Every unchanged neighborhood agrees up to translation with its old counterpart. The collision tests with a remote vertical owner use the full parity graph and are invariant under translation. Each possible local violation would therefore already have occurred. The original region margins, the interregion transitions, and the row-1 exterior persist. This argument combines all types on a single clean strip.

All early-color spans, residual profile lengths, margins, and original sea pieces together use \(O(sh^2+h^2)\) columns. The strip extends indefinitely in row 1, so it can be restricted to any width at least this large while including every retained double. Since \(2\delta<\varepsilon\), the constant in the width bound is absorbed by the unused logarithmic power for large \(h\). The row upper bound \(f(h,m)\le m+q_h\) finishes the result.
